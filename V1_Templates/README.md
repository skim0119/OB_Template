# V1 Templates

These templates are tailored for research-focused note-taking with emphasis on:
- Mathematical content with LaTeX support
- Clear linking between related notes
- Structured organization of complex research topics
- Support for both quick fleeting notes and more organized topic notes

## Templates Overview

1. **Research_Note**: For detailed research concepts with mathematical expressions
2. **Fleeting_Note**: Quick capture of ideas with potential connections
3. **Topic_Note**: Organization of related concepts in a specific domain
4. **Meeting_Note**: Documentation of discussions and action items
5. **Project_Note**: Tracking ongoing research projects
6. **Literature_Note**: Summarizing academic papers
7. **TODO_List**: Task management

## Usage Tips

- Use fleeting notes for quick capture of ideas
- Develop topic notes to organize related concepts
- Create project notes for tracking ongoing research work
- Use literature notes for paper summaries
- Implement meeting notes for collaboration records
